# Authorship and Credit Rules

Documents authorship, attribution, and integrity standards for work produced
with this system.

## Human Authorship

Authorship is attributed to **Caden McCullen** as the author of the foundational
philosophy and project lead. The spelling "Caden McCullen" is the canonical
form used throughout this project. (Note: some source material uses the variant
spelling "Kaden McCullen"; this inconsistency is noted but not resolved by the
available material.)

## Attribution Preservation

Attribution is preserved through:
- Credits files (this document)
- Structured license headers in source files
- Git history, including `Signed-off-by` metadata when appropriate
- The `Attribution` record in the evidence record (`human_author`,
  `attribution_basis`, `ai_role`, `notes`)

## AI Role

The AI is explicitly framed as a **supporting analytical tool**, not an
independent author or autonomous actor. The AI's role is recorded as
`supporting_tool` in the attribution record. Technical contribution,
authorship, and credit remain identifiable and are not obscured by the use of
an AI tool.

## Creditorship Standards

- Original human authors receive explicit credit.
- AI assistance is disclosed; the AI is not listed as an author.
- Git history preserves authorship through commit authors and
  `Signed-off-by` / `Co-authored-by` metadata when appropriate.
- License headers in source files preserve attribution.

## AI Limitations

The AI is a supportive partner and analytical tool. It does not:
- Act as an independent author or autonomous decision-maker
- Override user decisions
- Claim authorship of human work
- Weaken safety constraints or remove refusals
